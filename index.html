    How to read this board

    All times are departure-port local time. Singapore → Batam is shown in SG time (UTC+8); Batam → Singapore in WIB (UTC+7), exactly as the operators publish them. The clock and countdown follow the direction you picked, so a Batam countdown is never an hour off.

    Arrival times and durations are approximate — typical crossing times, not published arrivals. Use them for planning, not for connections.

    ⇄ shared marks interlined trips: Sindo and Batam Fast sell the same physical sailing, so don't count it as two boats. Batam Fast's suspended Gold Coast trips are excluded.

    Weekend timings also apply on public holidays. Sindo moves its HarbourFront services to the new terminal (70 m away) from 15 Jul 2026; schedules unchanged.

    Before you travel

    Check-in closes 35 minutes before departure — that's the cutoff the board shows. Immigration queues at HarbourFront and Batam Centre can be long at weekends and holidays, so get there well before the cutoff.

    Location detection uses your device position where the browser allows it and falls back to your timezone. If you're in neither place it defaults to Singapore. Flip the direction any time by tapping the route.

    Schedules change without notice — confirm with the operator for your travel date. Sources: Batam Fast (WEF 11 Apr 2026), Majestic (WEF 12 May 2026), Sindo and Horizon (current).
